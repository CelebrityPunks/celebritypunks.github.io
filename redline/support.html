Support

Help for Redline: Kegel Trainer for Men, published by Ibrahim Haddad.

Contact us

Email bob.haddad.33@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

Free and Pro

Program days 1–3 and every Foundation exercise are free. Redline Pro unlocks the full 84-day program and every Builder and Advanced exercise. You can choose a yearly plan, which includes a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, a monthly plan, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices and renewal terms are shown in the app before you buy.

Free trial

You aren't charged during the 3-day trial. If notifications are on, Redline reminds you the day before the trial ends. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Restore a purchase

Sign in with the Apple Account you bought with, connect to the internet, and tap Profile → Restore purchases. If Pro still doesn't appear, email us.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Redline. You can also tap Profile → Manage subscription in the app. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your plan

Redline builds your plan from your assessment. Change your daily training time in Profile, or tap Retake assessment to rebuild the plan from new answers. Every seventh day is a lighter recovery day.

Voice coach and reminders

Choose Mia, Cole or Off in Profile → Voice coach. If you can't hear the coach, check your volume and make sure the coach isn't set to Off. If reminders don't arrive, turn on Profile → Daily reminder and allow notifications for Redline in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Using the 3D model

Drag the figure to rotate it, and tap a muscle to see what it does. On an exercise page, tap Pelvic view for a close-up of the pelvic floor.

Reset progress

Profile → Reset progress clears your workout history and streak. It does not cancel a subscription.

Safety

Redline offers general fitness guidance and is not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain. See a doctor or pelvic floor physiotherapist before starting if you have pain, a medical condition, urinary symptoms or recent surgery.
