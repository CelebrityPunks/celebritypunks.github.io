Privacy policy

Redline: Kegel Trainer for Men · Effective October 13, 2026

Redline is an iPhone fitness app published by Ibrahim Haddad. It teaches pelvic floor exercises with 3D anatomy, a training plan and a voice coach. Redline is built so your training stays private.

What we collect

Nothing. Redline has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no cross-app tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs. The app does not send anything to us, because there is no Redline server.

What stays on your iPhone

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Your assessment answers: goals, age range, Kegel experience, how long you can hold a squeeze, and activity level
  • Plan settings: daily training time, reminder time and coach voice
  • Workout history: the dates you trained, how long, and which exercises
  • Preferences such as haptic cues and reminders

This information is never sent to us or to third parties. Redline does not access your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, location or Apple Health.

Voice coach

Coach voices are recorded clips included in the app. If a line isn't included, iOS speaks it on your device. No text or audio leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

Apple processes Redline Pro subscriptions and purchases through the App Store. The app uses Apple's StoreKit on your device to confirm access and restore purchases. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. Read Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles App Store transactions.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Redline schedules a daily training reminder and, if you start a free trial, a reminder the day before it ends. Both are scheduled on your device. You can turn reminders off in Profile or in iOS Settings.

Your controls

Use Profile → Reset progress to clear your workout history, or Retake assessment to change your answers. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Device backups, such as iCloud Backup, are managed by Apple and your device settings. Deleting data or the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account.

Age

Redline is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 16.

Support email and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and we use it only to reply. This website has no analytics or advertising scripts. Its host, GitHub Pages, may process request logs such as IP addresses for hosting and security.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Publisher: Ibrahim Haddad. For privacy questions, email bob.haddad.33@gmail.com.
