Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 13, 2026

Binaural Mind ("the App") is developed by Ibrahim Haddad. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

Binaural Mind does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App runs entirely on your device.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use any analytics services, advertising frameworks, or third-party tracking tools.

Advertising

The App may present rewarded ads that users can choose to watch in exchange for temporary in-app access. Ad delivery is handled by Google AdMob. Please review Google's policies for details on how ad services may process limited technical data for delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement.

Session History

Session history data (such as session duration and preset used) is stored locally on your device only. This data is never sent to any server or third party.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The App does not have access to your payment information. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles purchase transactions.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no data is collected directly by the app, the app itself does not store personal data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted here with an updated effective date.
