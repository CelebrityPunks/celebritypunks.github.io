Common Help

  • If a purchased sound pack is missing, open the app and use Restore Purchases.
  • If a rewarded unlock does not apply, make sure the ad fully completes before returning to the app.
  • If audio stops unexpectedly, reopen the app and start a new session.
  • For App Store billing or refund requests, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
